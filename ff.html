About Kagorogoro Vocational School

Established in 1995, Kagorogoro Vocational School has been a beacon of practical education in the heart of East Africa. We specialize in hands-on training that prepares students for immediate employment in various trades.

Our motto "Skills for Life" reflects our commitment to empowering youth with practical knowledge that transforms communities. With our experienced instructors and modern workshops, we bridge the gap between education and employment.

Our Programs

  • Masonry and Construction - 2 year diploma
  • Automotive Mechanics - 18 month certificate
  • Tailoring and Fashion Design - 1 year certificate
  • Catering and Hospitality - 1 year certificate
  • Electrical Installation - 2 year diploma
